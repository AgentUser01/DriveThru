Beginner's Guide to Vaping: Everything You Need to Know

New to vaping? This comprehensive guide covers everything from choosing your first device to understanding nicotine levels, maintenance tips, and safety considerations.

📰

What is Vaping?

Vaping is the act of inhaling vapor produced by an electronic device called a vaporizer or e-cigarette. Unlike traditional cigarettes that burn tobacco, vaping devices heat a liquid (called e-liquid or vape juice) to create an aerosol that users inhale. This process is often considered a less harmful alternative to smoking, though it's important to note that vaping is not completely risk-free.

The e-liquid typically contains propylene glycol (PG), vegetable glycerin (VG), flavorings, and optionally nicotine. When heated by the device's coil, this liquid transforms into the vapor you inhale.

Types of Vaping Devices

There are several types of vaping devices available, each suited to different preferences and experience levels:

1. Disposable Vapes

Pre-filled, pre-charged devices that you use until the e-liquid runs out, then dispose of. Perfect for beginners who want to try vaping without commitment. Popular brands include Elf Bar, STLTH, and Vuse Go.

2. Pod Systems

Compact, rechargeable devices that use pre-filled or refillable pods. They're easy to use, portable, and offer a satisfying experience. JUUL, Vuse Alto, and STLTH are popular pod systems we carry at Drive Thru.

3. Vape Pens

Cylindrical devices slightly larger than pod systems. They typically have refillable tanks and replaceable coils, offering more customization while remaining beginner-friendly.

4. Box Mods

Advanced devices with adjustable settings like wattage and temperature control. These are recommended for experienced vapers who want maximum customization.

Understanding Nicotine Levels

E-liquids come in various nicotine strengths, measured in milligrams per milliliter (mg/mL) or as a percentage:

  • 0mg (0%) – No nicotine. Great for those who've quit nicotine but enjoy vaping.
  • 3mg (0.3%) – Very low. Suitable for light smokers or those stepping down.
  • 6mg (0.6%) – Low to medium. Good for moderate smokers.
  • 12mg (1.2%) – Medium. Suitable for regular smokers.
  • 20mg (2%) – High (salt nicotine). Common in pod systems for heavy smokers.
  • 50mg (5%) – Very high (salt nicotine). For heavy smokers transitioning from cigarettes.

If you're switching from cigarettes, start with a nicotine level that matches your smoking habits. Our staff at Drive Thru can help you choose the right strength.

Choosing Your First Device

For beginners, we recommend starting with either a disposable vape or a simple pod system. Here's why:

  • Simplicity – No settings to adjust, just inhale and go
  • Portability – Compact and easy to carry
  • Low maintenance – Minimal cleaning required
  • Affordable – Lower upfront cost to try vaping
  • Satisfying nicotine delivery – Designed to mimic the throat hit of cigarettes

Basic Maintenance Tips

Even simple devices benefit from basic care:

  • Keep your device clean and dry
  • Store upright to prevent leaking
  • Don't let your pod or tank run completely dry
  • Charge your device before the battery dies completely
  • Replace pods or coils when flavor diminishes (usually every 1-2 weeks)

Safety Considerations

⚠️ Important: Vaping products contain nicotine, which is highly addictive. These products are intended for adult smokers only and are not recommended for non-smokers or youth.

Additional safety tips:

  • Only purchase from reputable retailers like Drive Thru
  • Never modify your device or use damaged batteries
  • Keep e-liquids away from children and pets
  • If you experience any adverse effects, stop using and consult a healthcare provider
