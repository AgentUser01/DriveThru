How Our Drive-Thru Works

Fast, convenient service without leaving your vehicle

It's Simple & Fast

1

🚗 Drive Up

Pull up to our drive-through window. Look for the clearly marked "Drive Thru" lane at our Kahnawake location. Our friendly staff will greet you promptly.

2

🆔 Verify Your Age

Present a valid government-issued ID showing you are 18 years of age or older. This is a mandatory requirement for all tobacco and vaping product purchases in Quebec.

3

🛒 Place Your Order

Tell our staff what products you need. Not sure what you want? Our knowledgeable team can make recommendations based on your preferences and experience level.

4

💳 Pay & Collect

Pay with cash or card. We accept all major credit and debit cards. Collect your purchase, and you're on your way!

Frequently Asked Questions

What are your drive-through hours?

Our drive-through operates during all business hours: Open daily 8 AM - 10 PM, Monday through Sunday.

Do I need to call ahead?

No appointment necessary! Just drive up when it's convenient for you. However, you're welcome to call ahead at (450) 290-0340 if you want to ensure we have a specific product in stock.

What forms of payment do you accept?

We accept cash, debit cards, and all major credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express).

Can I return items purchased through the drive-through?

Yes! Our return policy applies to all purchases, regardless of how they were made. Please see our Returns & Refunds policy for details.

Is there a minimum purchase required?

No minimum purchase required. Whether you need one item or many, we're happy to serve you!
