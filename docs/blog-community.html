Drive Thru Sponsors Local Kahnawake Hockey Team

We're proud to support our community's youth sports programs. Announcing our sponsorship of the Kahnawake Warriors minor hockey team.

🏒

At Drive Thru, we believe in giving back to the community that has supported us since day one. That's why we're thrilled to announce our official sponsorship of the Kahnawake Warriors youth hockey program for the 2024-2025 season.

More Than Just Business

Kahnawake isn't just where we do business—it's home. Many of our team members grew up playing hockey on local rinks, and we know firsthand how important youth sports are for building character, teamwork, and community bonds.

"Hockey taught me discipline and gave me lifelong friends," says Mike, our store manager. "Sponsoring these kids is our way of making sure the next generation has the same opportunities we had."

What Our Sponsorship Provides

Our sponsorship helps cover essential costs that make youth hockey accessible to all families in our community:

  • New jerseys and equipment for the Peewee and Bantam divisions
  • Ice time fees at the Kahnawake Sports Complex
  • Tournament registration for regional competitions
  • Transportation costs for away games
  • Year-end celebration for players and families

A Message to Young Athletes

To all the young players on the Warriors: we're cheering for you! Whether you dream of the NHL or just love being on the ice with your friends, keep working hard, supporting your teammates, and having fun.

Remember that success isn't just about winning games—it's about the effort you put in, the friendships you make, and the person you become along the way.

🏒 Go Warriors! 🏒

Catch the Kahnawake Warriors in action at the Kahnawake Sports Complex. Check the community calendar for game schedules!

Our Commitment to Kahnawake

This sponsorship is just one part of our ongoing commitment to Kahnawake. We also support:

  • Local charity events and fundraisers
  • Community festivals and celebrations
  • Employment opportunities for local residents
  • Partnerships with other local businesses

We're grateful for every customer who chooses to shop with us. Your support allows us to reinvest in programs like this that make our community stronger.

Get Involved

Want to support youth sports in Kahnawake? Here are some ways you can help:

  • Attend games and cheer on the teams
  • Volunteer as a coach or assistant
  • Donate equipment your kids have outgrown
  • Support local businesses that sponsor youth programs

Thank You, Kahnawake!

Your continued support of Drive Thru makes initiatives like this possible. We're proud to be part of this community.
