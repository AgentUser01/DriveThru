Quebec Vaping Regulations: What You Need to Know

Stay informed about provincial regulations on vaping products, age restrictions, and where you can legally vape in Quebec.

⚖️

Disclaimer: This article provides general information about vaping regulations in Quebec. Regulations may change. Always verify current laws with official government sources.

Age Restrictions

In Quebec, you must be 18 years or older to purchase, possess, or use vaping products. This applies to:

  • E-cigarettes and vape devices
  • E-liquids (with or without nicotine)
  • Vaping accessories
  • All tobacco products

Retailers are required by law to verify age before selling these products. At Drive Thru, we take this responsibility seriously and always check ID.

Where You Can (and Can't) Vape

Quebec's Tobacco Control Act applies similar restrictions to vaping as to smoking. You cannot vape in:

Prohibited Areas:

  • Indoor public spaces (malls, restaurants, bars, offices)
  • Workplaces
  • Public transit vehicles and stations
  • Within 9 meters of building entrances
  • School grounds and daycare facilities
  • Healthcare facilities and hospitals
  • Sports venues and arenas
  • Outdoor patios of restaurants and bars
  • Parks and playgrounds (in many municipalities)

Where Vaping is Generally Permitted:

  • Private residences (your own home)
  • Private vehicles (when alone or with consenting adults)
  • Outdoors in areas without specific restrictions
  • Designated vaping areas (where available)
⚠️ Note: Individual municipalities may have additional restrictions. When in doubt, ask or refrain from vaping in public spaces.

Product Regulations

Nicotine Concentration

Federal regulations limit nicotine concentration in vaping products to 20mg/mL (2%) in most retail settings. However, some products with higher concentrations may be available through specialty retailers or specific jurisdictions.

Flavored Products

As of recent updates, Quebec and federal regulations restrict certain flavored vaping products to reduce appeal to youth. Tobacco and menthol flavors remain widely available. For the latest on flavor availability, check with your local retailer.

Packaging and Labeling

All vaping products sold in Canada must include:

  • Health warnings on packaging
  • Nicotine content information
  • Ingredient lists
  • Child-resistant packaging for e-liquids

Advertising Restrictions

Vaping product advertising is heavily restricted in Canada:

  • No advertising that could appeal to youth
  • No lifestyle advertising
  • No health claims
  • Limited to adult-only venues and age-gated digital platforms
  • No testimonials or endorsements

Purchasing Online

Online sales of vaping products are legal but regulated. Retailers must:

  • Verify customer age before purchase
  • Ensure age verification upon delivery
  • Comply with all packaging and labeling requirements

Penalties for Violations

Violations of vaping regulations can result in significant fines:

  • Selling to minors: Fines up to $125,000 for repeat offenses
  • Vaping in prohibited areas: Fines starting at $100-$500
  • Advertising violations: Substantial corporate penalties

Our Commitment to Compliance

At Drive Thru, we're committed to full compliance with all federal and provincial regulations. We:

  • Verify age for every purchase
  • Only sell products that meet Canadian safety standards
  • Provide proper health warnings and information
  • Stay updated on regulatory changes
  • Train our staff on compliance requirements
