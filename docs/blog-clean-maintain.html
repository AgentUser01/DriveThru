How to Clean and Maintain Your Vape Device

Regular cleaning extends your device's life and improves flavor. Learn the proper techniques for keeping your vape in top condition.

🔧

Why Cleaning Your Vape Matters

A clean vape device isn't just about hygiene—it directly affects your vaping experience. E-liquid residue builds up over time, leading to muted flavors, poor vapor production, and even device malfunction. Regular maintenance ensures you get the best possible taste and extends the lifespan of your investment.

What You'll Need

  • Paper towels or clean cloth
  • Cotton swabs (Q-tips)
  • Warm water
  • Isopropyl alcohol (for deep cleaning)
  • Small bowl
  • Toothbrush (soft bristle, optional)

Step-by-Step Cleaning Guide

Step 1: Disassemble Your Device

Carefully take apart your vape into its components: tank, coil, drip tip, and battery/mod. Never submerge the battery or electronic components in water.

Step 2: Empty and Rinse the Tank

Pour out any remaining e-liquid. Rinse the tank under warm running water for 30 seconds. For stubborn residue, let it soak in warm water for 10-15 minutes.

Step 3: Clean the Drip Tip

The drip tip (mouthpiece) collects the most bacteria. Soak it in warm soapy water, scrub gently with a cotton swab, and rinse thoroughly.

Step 4: Wipe Down the Connections

Use a cotton swab lightly dampened with isopropyl alcohol to clean the 510 connection (where the tank meets the battery). This removes e-liquid residue and ensures proper electrical contact.

Step 5: Dry Everything Completely

Let all components air dry for at least an hour, or gently pat dry with paper towels. Never reassemble with wet parts—this can damage electronics and dilute your e-liquid.

Step 6: Reassemble and Prime

Put your device back together. If you installed a new coil, prime it by adding a few drops of e-liquid directly to the cotton and waiting 5-10 minutes before vaping.

How Often Should You Clean?

  • Weekly: Quick rinse of tank and drip tip
  • Every 1-2 weeks: Full cleaning as described above
  • When changing flavors: Always clean to prevent flavor mixing
  • When you notice issues: Muted flavor, gurgling, or leaking

Coil Maintenance

Coils can't be fully cleaned and need regular replacement. Signs your coil needs changing:

  • Burnt or off taste
  • Less vapor production
  • Dark, gunky appearance
  • Gurgling sounds

Most coils last 1-3 weeks depending on usage. Visit Drive Thru for replacement coils—we carry options for all major devices.

Pro Tips for Device Longevity

  • Store your device upright to prevent leaking
  • Keep the battery contacts clean
  • Don't overcharge your battery
  • Use the correct charger for your device
  • Avoid extreme temperatures
  • Don't let your tank run completely dry
