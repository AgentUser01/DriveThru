Top 10 E-Liquid Flavors for 2025

Discover the most popular flavors this year, from classic tobacco to exotic fruit blends. Find your new favorite!

🍓

The vaping industry continues to innovate with exciting new flavor profiles. Whether you're a fan of fruity, dessert, menthol, or classic tobacco tastes, there's something for everyone. Here are the top 10 e-liquid flavors flying off our shelves at Drive Thru this year.

1. 🥭 Mango Blast

Tropical mango remains the undisputed king of vape flavors. This sweet, juicy flavor offers a smooth, exotic taste that's perfect all year round. Our Mango Blast 60ml is our #1 seller.

Best for: Fruit lovers, all-day vaping

2. 🍓 Strawberry Ice

Sweet strawberries meet cool menthol for a refreshing combination. The icy finish makes this a favorite during summer, but fans enjoy it year-round for its crisp, clean taste.

Best for: Menthol fans who want fruity sweetness

3. 💙 Blue Raspberry

Inspired by the classic candy flavor, blue raspberry delivers a tart, sweet punch with nostalgic appeal. Our Blue Raspberry Ice variant adds a cooling sensation for extra refreshment.

Best for: Candy lovers, sweet tooth vapers

4. 🍉 Watermelon Mint

Juicy watermelon paired with fresh mint creates a summery vibe in every puff. Light and refreshing, it's become a go-to for vapers who want something not too sweet.

Best for: Light, refreshing all-day vape

5. 🍇 Grape Soda

This flavor captures the fizzy, sweet taste of your favorite grape soda. With a slightly effervescent sensation, it's fun and unique among fruit flavors.

Best for: Soda fans, unique flavor seekers

6. 🍦 Vanilla Custard

Rich, creamy, and indulgent. Vanilla custard is the top dessert flavor, offering a smooth, sweet profile with hints of caramel. Perfect for after-dinner vaping.

Best for: Dessert lovers, evening sessions

7. 🌿 Classic Menthol

Pure, clean menthol never goes out of style. For those who loved menthol cigarettes, this provides that familiar cooling sensation with a smooth throat hit.

Best for: Ex-menthol smokers, clean palate seekers

8. 🍑 Peach Ring

Inspired by the popular gummy candy, peach ring flavor combines sweet peach with a candy-like finish. It's like vaping your favorite childhood treat.

Best for: Candy enthusiasts, sweet flavor lovers

9. ☕ Café Mocha

Coffee and chocolate unite in this sophisticated flavor. Café mocha offers a rich, aromatic experience that's perfect for your morning vape or coffee break.

Best for: Coffee lovers, morning vapers

10. 🍂 Classic Tobacco

For those transitioning from cigarettes, classic tobacco flavor provides familiar comfort. Modern tobacco e-liquids offer a smoother, cleaner version of the taste you know.

Best for: Ex-smokers, traditional taste seekers

How to Choose Your Flavor

Not sure where to start? Here are some tips:

  • Ex-smokers: Start with tobacco or menthol, then explore from there
  • Sweet tooth: Try dessert or candy-inspired flavors
  • Refreshing vibe: Fruit + menthol combinations are your friend
  • All-day vape: Lighter flavors like watermelon or mango won't overwhelm
